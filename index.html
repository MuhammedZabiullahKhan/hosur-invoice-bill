About & Instructions

⚠️ IMPORTANT WARNING:

  • • Clearing browser cache will DELETE all unsaved invoices
  • • Always save PDFs to your device/Downloads folder
  • • We do NOT store your data on any external server
  • • You are solely responsible for backing up your invoices
  • • PDF files are your permanent legal record

📖 HOW TO USE:

  1. Enter your Business Name and Contact details
  2. Add Customer Name and Mobile Number
  3. Type Item Name (auto-suggestions appear from history)
  4. Enter Quantity and Price (total auto-calculates instantly)
  5. Click "Save Invoice & Preview" button
  6. Review the invoice in the preview window
  7. Click "Print as PDF" to save to your device
  8. Use "New Bill" button to clear form for next customer
  9. Filter invoices by date using the date picker
  10. View, Edit, or Delete past invoices from the list

🛠️ TECHNICAL DETAILS:

Your invoices are stored in your browser's IndexedDB database. PDF files must be saved manually. This is a 100% offline-first PWA that works without internet.
